name: BMU scenario prototype scope
description: Why the new Add/Edit/Send experience differs from the earlier capture-first demo.
---

The approved B1–B10 feedback governs the current Add/Edit/Send practice prototype. Home is action-first, with Add record foremost, limited review/send readiness, and recent notes; do not restore an analytics dashboard.

**Why:** The user asked to plan and execute the attached feedback in order. Its defaults update the earlier scenario specification while preserving the emphasis on establishing and correcting catch records.

**How to apply:** Keep the new prototype synthetic, English, local-only and isolated from legacy storage. Do not wire its photo, send or schedule controls to live AI, delivery, sync or background jobs. There is no new approval workflow. A later operational app needs an explicit decision before reusing prototype rules or migrating legacy records.

Processors weigh each species separately for each fisher. When a catch cannot be separated, they record a mixed category. Kg and Ksh are established together at weighing; a species has the same price throughout the landing day.

**Why:** The approved feedback explicitly records these as confirmed processor practices.

**How to apply:** Keep mixed catch a normal entry path, keep daily prices visible and editable, and do not add size or grade pricing classes without a new product decision.
