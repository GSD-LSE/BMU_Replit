---
name: BMU scenario prototype scope
description: Why the new Add/Edit/Send experience differs from the earlier capture-first demo.
---

The approved screen specification’s assumptions A1–A8 govern the current Add/Edit/Send prototype. They explicitly put To review and recent records on Home, despite the earlier action-only Home direction.

**Why:** The user chose a connected testable prototype of that specification, replacing the visible capture/voice/approval/Overview experience, not combining the two product experiments.

**How to apply:** Keep the new prototype synthetic, English, local-only and isolated from legacy storage. Do not wire its photo, send or schedule controls to live AI, delivery, sync or background jobs. There is no new approval workflow. A later operational app needs an explicit decision before reusing prototype rules or migrating legacy records.
